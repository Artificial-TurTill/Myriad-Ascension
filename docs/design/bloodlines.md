# Bloodlines

Bloodlines are permanent lineage commitments, not freely swappable equipment.

## General Player Acquisition

Players do not randomly spawn with special bloodlines.

A player can obtain one through specific progression sources such as:

- quest rewards,
- ancient cultivator tombs,
- monuments,
- auctions,
- spirit pacts,
- refinement of a compatible spirit-beast bloodline source.

NPC bloodlines can also be inherited through family lineage from birth.

## One Primary Lineage

A cultivator has one primary bloodline lineage.

Once established, it cannot simply be replaced by an unrelated beast lineage.

A stronger bloodline of the **same family/lineage** can overpower and overwrite a weaker version.

An incompatible lineage, such as attempting to force a Tiger bloodline over an established Tortoise bloodline, does not replace it. It causes bloodline conflict and damage instead.

This makes bloodline choice a long-term cultivation commitment.

## Source Beast and Grade

Bloodline quality is tied to the source beast.

The beast's cultivation rank/state when the bloodline source is obtained determines the grade ceiling.

A bloodline cannot be refined beyond the power represented by its source grade unless the cultivator later finds a stronger compatible source capable of overwriting it.

Only grades explicitly designed by the project should be added; higher-grade names should not be invented automatically from realm names.

## Purity

Purity and grade are separate.

The same nominal grade may be obtained at different purity depending on transfer method.

Current hierarchy of transfer quality:

1. natural last-breath transfer — highest potential purity,
2. spirit pact — very high purity,
3. NPC family inheritance — high but lineage-dependent purity,
4. killing/refining the beast — significantly reduced purity,
5. quest/tomb/monument/auction sources — purity determined by the specific reward/item.

Waiting until an ancient spirit beast naturally reaches its final breath can therefore be substantially more valuable than killing it.

## Spirit Pact

A spirit pact with a compatible beast can improve/refine the cultivator's matching bloodline.

Pacts are not generic bloodline conversion tools: a Tortoise pact improves a compatible Tortoise lineage rather than allowing arbitrary lineage replacement.

## Primordial Tortoise Bloodline

The first implemented lineage family is the **Primordial Tortoise Bloodline**.

### Earth Rank

The currently defined early-game grade is **Earth Rank**.

- effective power keeps pace through **Saint**,
- above Saint the bloodline remains present but no longer scales competitively without a stronger compatible lineage,
- the grade is not allowed to self-evolve beyond its source ceiling.

### Benefits

The bloodline changes the cultivator physically **internally**, but does not force visible skin/model changes.

Benefit hooks currently defined:

- lifespan,
- defense,
- regeneration,
- Earth compatibility,
- general resistance,
- compatibility with shell/tortoise defensive arts,
- breakthrough stability.

Exact numbers remain balance data rather than hard-coded lore.

### Primordialis Testudo Longevity Art

A Tortoise-family bloodline synergizes with the **Primordialis Testudo Longevity Art**.

The synergy improves cultivation efficiency according to bloodline purity while retaining the method's slow, foundation-focused identity.

## Rarity

Ancient Tortoise spirit beasts are intentionally extremely rare.

Initial world-generation target:

- approximately **5 ancient Tortoise candidates per 10,000 × 10,000 block area**,
- this is a statistical target, not a guarantee or regular spacing rule,
- players should not expect to encounter several within a normal 1,000-block exploration radius.

The world generator should use sparse regional placement rather than common mob spawning.

## Future Primordial Tortoise Legacy

A future inheritance is tied to an ancient era in which spirit beasts were the dominant and most powerful species.

That legacy reaches **9th-Rank Open Heaven** significance.

It is distinct from the ordinary Earth-Rank bloodline used in the early-world Testudo Clan.

The eventual inheritance should let a cultivator benefit from the Primordial Tortoise lineage even at the peak of Open Heaven rather than making the early Saint-capped grade artificially scale that far.
