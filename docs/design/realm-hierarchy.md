# Cultivation Realm Hierarchy

The internal progression hierarchy follows the requested Martial Peak-inspired subdivision structure.

## Pre-Cultivation

- Mortal

Mortal is an internal state rather than a cultivation realm. New players begin here with no usable Qi.

## Starting World

1. Tempered Body — 9 Stages
2. Initial Element — 9 Stages
3. Qi Transformation — 9 Stages
4. Separation and Reunion — 9 Stages
5. True Element — 9 Stages
6. Immortal Ascension — 9 Stages
7. Transcendent — 3 Orders

## Planet Level

8. Saint — 3 Orders

**Current planned implementation ceiling: Saint.**

## Starfield Level

9. Saint King — 3 Orders
10. Origin Returning — 3 Orders
11. Origin King — 3 Orders

The Planet -> Starfield transition is not an ascension. It is physical/interstellar travel using technologies or formations such as starships and teleport arrays.

## Star Boundary Level

12. Dao Source — 3 Orders
13. Emperor — 3 Orders
14. Pseudo-Great Emperor — special Emperor classification
15. Dao Seal — special Emperor classification

## Outer Universe Level

16. Half-Step Open Heaven — special Emperor classification
17. Open Heaven — 9 Ranks

## Beyond the Outer Universe / Multiverse

18. World Creation — no canonical minor subdivision currently encoded

## Subdivision Labels

The UI and progression code distinguish:

- **Stage** for the first six cultivation realms,
- **Order** from Transcendent through normal Emperor,
- **Rank** for Open Heaven,
- no fake minor rank for Pseudo-Great Emperor, Dao Seal, Half-Step Open Heaven, or World Creation.

## Tempered Body Bands

Tempered Body is split into three functional bands:

### Stages 1–3

- physical/body strengthening,
- vessel preparation,
- no active Qi.

### Stages 4–6

- body strengthening continues,
- ambient/world energy becomes perceptible,
- environmental elemental alignment can begin influencing attunement training.

### Stages 7–9

- natural ambient energy gathering begins,
- dantian/meridian preparation accelerates,
- the player is approaching Initial Element.

**Myriad Ascension override:** Tempered Body does not yet grant consciously usable Qi. Active Qi begins at Initial Element.

## Ascension

A large world-tier transition is normally an ascension, except Planet -> Starfield.

Ascension can later provide opportunities to sacrifice accumulated effort/resources for fundamental improvements such as affinity refinement, constitution changes, or other rebirth-style choices.

Ascension is also intended to gate access to higher-world materials, enemies, techniques, sects, structures, spiritual resources, and crafting tiers.

See `docs/design/martial-peak-progression-adaptation.md` for qualitative realm unlocks.
