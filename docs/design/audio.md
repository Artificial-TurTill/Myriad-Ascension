# Audio Asset Policy

The project owner supplies final sound and music assets.

The assistant/development process must **not synthesize replacement sounds**.

For temporary development, the supplied file `Placeholder.ogg` is approved as the single source sound. It may be copied and renamed to satisfy temporary sound-event paths until final assets are supplied.

Validated placeholder source:

- Container format: Ogg
- Codec: Vorbis
- Channels: stereo
- Sample rate: 48 kHz
- Duration: approximately 2 seconds

Temporary sound files should be clearly treated as placeholders and replaced without changing sound-event identifiers where practical.
