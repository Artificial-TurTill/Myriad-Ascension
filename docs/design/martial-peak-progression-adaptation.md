# Martial Peak Progression Adaptation

Myriad Ascension uses the requested Martial Peak-style realm subdivision structure and qualitative realm transitions.

This document records the **gameplay facts being adapted**, not prose from the source material.

Where Myriad Ascension has an explicit design rule that differs, the project rule wins.

## Tempered Body

### Stages 1–3 — Body Strengthening

Focus:

- physical strengthening,
- toughness,
- preparing the vessel,
- no active Qi use.

### Stages 4–6 — Energy Sensing

Focus:

- continued body tempering,
- progressively sensing ambient/world energy,
- learning to recognize aligned environments.

Myriad Ascension uses this band for early environmental affinity training.

### Stages 7–9 — Natural Gathering

Focus:

- the vessel starts naturally drawing in ambient energy,
- meridians/dantian are being prepared,
- transition toward true Qi usage.

**Project override:** this gathered pre-Qi energy is not consciously usable as active Qi during Tempered Body. Active Qi usage begins at Initial Element.

## Initial Element

- 9 Stages.
- First realm where usable internal Qi is properly available in Myriad Ascension.
- Qi can reinforce the body and power techniques.
- Qi is not yet freely projected away from the body.
- Elemental Qi arts become properly trainable here.

## Qi Transformation

- 9 Stages.
- Qi can be projected outside the body for attack/defense/techniques.
- Major increase in usable reserves/control demands.
- First realm where **cultivation can substitute for sleep** in Myriad Ascension.

### Sleep substitution

A player at Qi Transformation or above can satisfy the night's rest requirement through **60 real minutes of uninterrupted cultivation**.

In multiplayer, night advances only if every relevant player is either:

- sleeping normally, or
- has satisfied the cultivation-rest requirement.

Mortals and Tempered Body cultivators cannot substitute cultivation for sleep.

## Separation and Reunion

- 9 Stages.
- Stronger storage, control, and application of Qi.
- Major mental/path instability checkpoint.
- Myriad Ascension will connect this realm strongly to path discipline, temptation, Qi nature, and technique side effects.

## True Element

- 9 Stages.
- Qi is purified into a qualitatively stronger state.
- Supports more complex techniques.
- Natural cultivation-powered flight becomes available here.

## Immortal Ascension

- 9 Stages.
- Knowledge Sea / Divine Sense layer becomes available.
- Enables spiritual perception, soul storage/interactions, and later Divine Sense attacks.

## Transcendent

- 3 Orders.
- First three-order realm in the progression.
- Represents a qualitative transition beyond the earlier nine-stage structure.
- Primordialis Testudo Clan's complete inheritance currently ends at this realm.

## Saint

- 3 Orders.
- Current planned implementation ceiling.
- Represents the Planet-level cap in the first major development scope.

## Emperor

- 3 Orders as normal minor cultivation levels.

After normal 3rd Order Emperor, the project keeps the requested progression entries:

1. Pseudo-Great Emperor
2. Dao Seal
3. Half-Step Open Heaven

These are modeled as special Emperor-level classifications rather than fake nine-stage realms.

## Pseudo-Great Emperor

A special peak-Emperor classification with no ordinary minor stages.

## Dao Seal

A special post-peak-Emperor classification representing condensed Dao foundation.

## Half-Step Open Heaven

A special classification immediately preceding Open Heaven.

## Open Heaven

- 9 Ranks.
- Uses Rank rather than Stage/Order.
- The future implementation will tie promotion to the Yin-Yang + Five Element foundation/resources and higher-world mechanics.

## World Creation

- No minor subdivision is currently encoded.
- This remains the final planned realm until the project owner extends the cosmology.
