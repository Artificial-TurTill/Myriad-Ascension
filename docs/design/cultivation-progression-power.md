# Myriad Ascension — Cultivation Progression, Power, Lifespan, and Ability Specification

> Status: authoritative design specification for realm progression.
>
> This document separates **Martial Peak source anchors** from **Myriad Ascension gameplay implementation**. Where Martial Peak explicitly defines a qualitative transition, the mod should preserve it. Numeric balancing values, Minecraft mechanics, power indices, lifespan targets, stat formulas, and acquisition loops are Myriad Ascension design values unless explicitly stated otherwise.

---

# 1. Fundamental Progression Rule

Cultivation is not a single XP bar.

Every advancement is the result of several independent axes:

1. **Body/Foundation** — can the vessel survive the next level?
2. **Energy Quantity** — has enough energy been accumulated?
3. **Energy Quality** — has the current energy been purified/evolved where required?
4. **Control** — can the cultivator actually manipulate what they possess?
5. **Comprehension** — does the cultivator understand the next qualitative step?
6. **Soul/Will** — can the Divine Soul, will, or consciousness support the transition?
7. **World Permission** — does the current world/dimension support the next level?
8. **Method Support** — does the active cultivation method reach the target realm?
9. **Purity/Stability** — are impurities, injuries, Qi deviation, contamination, or unstable foundations too severe?
10. **Realm-Specific Requirement** — World Baptism, Shi, Domain, Principles, Dao Seal, elemental condensation, Small Universe, etc.

A player can therefore have 100% ordinary Cultivation Progress and still be unable to break through.

# 2. Energy Evolution

The energy resource must change its identity with the realm.

## Ambient World Energy

This is environmental energy, not a player-owned resource.

Sources:

- natural world background
- spirit veins
- elemental terrain
- spirit stones
- formations
- treasure lands
- special dimensions

A Mortal cannot use it.

## Yuan Qi

**Begins forming naturally at Tempered Body Stage 7.**

State:

- generated by absorbing World Energy into dantian and meridians
- naturally accumulated at Tempered Body 7–9
- consciously usable from Initial Element
- projectable outside the body from Qi Transformation
- remains the primary internal energy through Separation and Reunion

## True Qi

At the transition to **True Element**, Yuan Qi is purified into the much stronger and more versatile True Qi.

Unlocks:

- higher efficiency
- more complex Secret Techniques
- true cultivation-powered flight
- stronger body reinforcement
- stronger technique conduction

True Qi remains the primary energy through:

- True Element
- Immortal Ascension
- Transcendent

## Saint Qi

To enter **Saint**, True Qi must be purified into Saint Qi.

Saint Qi remains the primary energy through:

- Saint
- Saint King
- Origin Returning
- Origin King

It also participates in forming **Shi** together with Will and Divine Soul Strength.

## Source Qi

To enter **Dao Source**, Saint Qi must be completely transformed into Source Qi.

Source Qi:

- is qualitatively superior to Saint Qi
- supports the use of World Principles
- is required for Dao Source cultivation
- becomes the precursor to Emperor Qi

## Emperor Qi

At **Emperor**, Source Qi begins converting into Emperor Qi.

A cultivator may enter Emperor before the conversion is absolutely complete, but cannot display the true full power of the realm until the conversion has matured.

The purest Emperor Qi is associated with a fully developed Emperor foundation/Dao Seal progression.

## Embryonic World Force

At **Half-Step Open Heaven**, after sufficient Yin-Yang + Five Element powers are condensed into the Dao Seal, the mutually promoting cycle generates an embryonic form of World Force.

This is not yet a complete Small Universe.

## World Force

At **Open Heaven**, the cultivator forms a Small Universe.

The primary power source ceases to be a normal dantian-style Qi pool.

The cultivator draws upon the **World Force generated by their Small Universe**.

## World Creation Power

World Creation is not represented as merely "higher World Force."

The source describes this level through mastery of the myriad Grand Daos and the ability to create true Universe Worlds and life.

For Myriad Ascension, World Creation should therefore use a distinct endgame power state:

- Perfect/Myriad Dao Power
- Creation Authority
- Universe Creation

Exact naming remains implementation-facing until final localization is chosen.

# 3. Evolving Character Systems

Core stats always have a hard numerical minimum of **1**, but their *meaning and functional layer* can evolve.

## Strength

Progression:

Mortal Strength
→ Tempered Physical Strength
→ Qi-Reinforced Strength
→ True-Qi Physical Strength
→ Transcendent Body Strength
→ Saint Body Strength
→ Source-Reinforced Body
→ Emperor Physique
→ World-Force Body

Strength is not the same as attack damage.

It affects:

- unarmed force
- weapon acceleration
- carry/training load
- knockback
- jumping
- grappling
- physical technique scaling
- how much artifact mass/recoil can be handled

## Vitality

Affects:

- maximum health
- injury tolerance
- healing rate
- organ/body resilience
- poison resistance
- blood loss
- recovery debt
- lifespan preservation
- ability to survive breakthrough backlash

## Agility

Affects:

- movement acceleration
- reaction
- attack speed limits
- dodge
- footwork
- aerial control
- flight handling
- ability to meaningfully wield a high-rank weapon

## Spiritual Sense

Evolution:

1. Mortal sensory instinct
2. World Energy Sense — Tempered Body 4+
3. refined energy perception
4. Divine Sense — Immortal Ascension
5. Shi perception
6. Domain perception
7. Principle perception — Dao Source+
8. World perception — Open Heaven+

The same stat remains numerically represented, but its unlocked functions change.

## Meridian Quality

Evolution:

Dormant pathways
→ opening/preparation
→ Yuan Qi channels
→ reinforced channels
→ True Qi channels
→ Saint-grade channels
→ Source-grade circulation network
→ Emperor-grade foundation

Affects:

- maximum safe Power %
- circulation speed
- technique throughput
- Burst safety
- internal injury risk
- conversion efficiency

## Dantian Quality

Evolution:

Dormant
→ forming reservoir
→ Yuan Qi reservoir
→ True Qi reservoir
→ Saint Qi reservoir
→ Source foundation
→ Emperor foundation
→ Dao Seal-centered foundation
→ superseded by Small Universe as primary power engine

## Soul Strength

Evolution:

Mortal Soul
→ tempered consciousness
→ Divine Soul / Knowledge Sea support
→ stronger Divine Sense
→ Shi integration
→ Domain support
→ Principle comprehension
→ Dao Seal stability
→ Small Universe consciousness/world control

## Derived systems

These are not simple core stats:

- Vessel Purity
- Impurity Load
- Battle Comprehension
- Cultivation Comprehension
- Dao Attainment
- Willpower
- Lifespan
- Bloodline
- Physique
- Technique Mastery
- Energy Purity
- Energy Capacity
- Energy Control
- World/Principle Affinity

# 4. Elemental Affinity Model

Affinity must never become a permanent hard class lock.

## Innate Affinity

Birth/genesis value.

Initial classical profile:

- Wood
- Fire
- Earth
- Metal
- Water
- Yin
- Yang

Starting rules remain:

- 10 random bonus points distributed among Five Elements + matching polarity
- Male: Yang receives baseline advantage and can receive bonus points; Yin cannot
- Female: Yin receives baseline advantage and can receive bonus points; Yang cannot
- starter values cap at 10

Innate Affinity normally does not change.

Exceptional systems may change it:

- ascension sacrifice
- rebirth
- bloodline transformation
- supreme treasures
- body reconstruction

## Current Attunement

Begins equal to Innate Affinity.

May improve through:

- aligned training
- elemental resources
- techniques
- environment
- spirit veins
- bloodlines
- treasures
- repeated safe exposure

Current Attunement affects efficiency rather than absolute permission.

Suggested interpretation:

- 0: no natural aptitude
- 1–2: poor
- 3–4: weak
- 5–6: average/decent
- 7–8: strong
- 9–10: exceptional starting-world aptitude
- 11–20: cultivated/refined affinity
- 21–40: profound affinity
- 41–60: Dao-aligned affinity
- 61–80: Principle-grade affinity
- 81–100: extreme worldly affinity
- >100: exceptional/otherworldly states

## What affinity modifies

- sensing speed
- natural absorption compatibility
- technique learning
- technique efficiency
- Qi conversion
- elemental resistance
- breakthrough stability
- resource refinement
- Dao comprehension
- Open Heaven elemental-resource safety

It never means:

> "You have Fire 0, therefore Fire cultivation is permanently impossible."

Instead:

> "Fire 0 means this path is extremely inefficient and dangerous until you create compatibility."

## Combat suppression

Actual combat uses power-gap rules.

Suggested effective gap behavior:

- same minor realm: equipment, stats, mastery, bloodline, purity and skill dominate
- +1 minor stage/order: noticeable but manageable advantage
- +2–3 minor stages: strong advantage
- peak previous major realm vs fresh next realm: next realm normally dominates
- one full major realm: overwhelming unless exceptional factors exist
- multiple major realms: ordinary attacks may fail to meaningfully injure the target

Factors capable of bridging gaps:

- vastly superior artifact
- bloodline
- Divine Spirit lineage
- physique
- formation
- superior method
- rare Secret Technique
- environmental advantage
- numbers/teamwork
- poison/sealing/soul attacks
- preparation
- sacrifice/burning lifespan
- exceptional mastery

A high-grade weapon does **not** grant high-grade body speed or reaction.

# 8. Tempered Body — 9 Stages

Source anchor:

- Stages 1–3 strengthen the body
- Stages 4–6 unlock increasing World Energy sense
- Stages 7–9 naturally gather World Energy and generate Yuan Qi
- ordinary cultivators at 7–9 cannot consciously accelerate absorption/restoration

## Stage 1 — Initial Tempering

Unlocks:

- cultivation-grade physical training
- Strength/Vitality/Agility begin exceeding mortal limits
- Body Foundation bar
- Training Fatigue

Gameplay:

- stance training
- load carrying
- striking post
- controlled repetition
- recovery

No Qi.

## Stage 2 — Tendon/Bone Adaptation

Unlocks:

- increased physical durability
- improved knockback resistance
- higher safe training load
- reduced mundane exhaustion

No Qi.

## Stage 3 — Completed Basic Vessel

Unlocks:

- body capable of surviving World Energy perception
- significantly superhuman physical baseline
- preparation for sensory transition

No Qi.

## Stage 4 — First World Energy Sense

Unlocks:

- vague perception of ambient World Energy
- Spiritual Sense gains its first cultivation-specific function
- environmental hints

Cannot absorb intentionally.

## Stage 5 — Directional Sense

Unlocks:

- relative direction toward stronger energy
- crude compatible/incompatible feeling
- better sensing of spirit stones and elemental locations

Cannot absorb intentionally.

## Stage 6 — Energy Identification

Unlocks:

- broad classification of World Energy
- elemental sensing
- stronger perception of veins/treasures/cultivators

Preparation for natural absorption.

## Stage 7 — Yuan Qi Generation Begins

Major unlock:

- natural World Energy absorption
- dantian becomes an actual reservoir
- meridians carry Yuan Qi
- **Yuan Qi stat/resource appears**

Important:

- reserve exists
- active technique use remains locked
- G does not accelerate normal absorption
- Power % remains locked

Environment now strongly matters.

## Stage 8 — Meridian/Dantian Expansion

Unlocks:

- larger Yuan Qi reserve
- stronger natural refill
- better internal stability
- preparation for deliberate circulation

Still cannot freely use Yuan Qi.

## Stage 9 — Consolidated Tempered Vessel

Unlocks:

- mature Tempered Body foundation
- high Yuan Qi storage relative to the realm
- breakthrough readiness

Requirements for Initial Element:

- body foundation complete
- stable dantian
- meridians prepared
- sufficient Yuan Qi
- method comprehension
- low enough injury/impurity

# 9. Initial Element — 9 Stages

Source anchor:

Cultivators now possess enough Yuan Qi to use Martial Skills, but their Yuan Qi cannot yet freely leave the body.

## Core unlock

**Conscious Yuan Qi use.**

The HUD now shows:

- Qi
- Power %

Controls:

- G: consciously circulate/mobilize Yuan Qi
- H: suppress circulation
- R: Burst if unlocked/safe

## Allowed techniques

- body reinforcement
- internal movement enhancement
- contact-based strikes
- weapon conduction while in physical contact
- defensive reinforcement
- circulation techniques

Not normally allowed:

- detached Qi projectile
- free-standing Qi shield away from body
- sustained external aura attack

## Progression

Stages increase:

- reserve
- meridian throughput
- dantian control
- safe Power %
- technique efficiency

By Stage 9:

- Yuan Qi control is mature
- reserve is ready for the Qi Transformation leap

# 10. Qi Transformation — 9 Stages

Source anchor:

- major increase in Yuan Qi
- Yuan Qi can finally be released outside the body
- first World Baptism
- initial instability/state-of-mind problems are possible

## Breakthrough event

World Baptism.

Checks:

- reserve
- control
- willpower
- body
- purity
- method
- injuries

## Core unlock

**External Yuan Qi projection.**

Techniques now include:

- ranged Qi attacks
- external barriers
- projected weapon aura
- external reinforcement
- Qi pressure
- remote technique effects

## Power system

Power % becomes much more dangerous.

Burst can:

- dramatically increase output
- rapidly increase meridian load
- destabilize emotions/control at low mastery

## Stage progression

1–3:
- stabilize enlarged reserve
- learn projection

4–6:
- sustained external techniques
- stronger barriers/aura

7–9:
- efficient projection
- high-output combat
- preparation for deeper control

# 11. Separation and Reunion — 9 Stages

Source anchor:

This realm does not introduce a new named energy. It represents substantially stronger Yuan Qi quantity/control compared with Qi Transformation.

## Myriad Ascension implementation

Core theme:

**capacity + precision + internal/external coordination.**

Unlocks progressively:

- multiple simultaneous circulation routes
- stronger Power allocation
- more efficient projected techniques
- improved recovery
- reduced waste
- more precise weapon/footwork technique integration

This is the realm where poor foundations become increasingly obvious.

Impurities and bad pills reduce:

- throughput
- reserve quality
- control
- conversion readiness

## Peak requirement

To enter True Element:

- Yuan Qi must be purified/condensed
- foundation must tolerate a qualitative energy upgrade

# 12. True Element — 9 Stages

Source anchor:

Yuan Qi is purified into **True Qi**, which is more powerful/versatile and enables Secret Techniques and flight.

## Breakthrough transformation

Yuan Qi → True Qi.

Conversion must be represented as an actual process.

A player may temporarily hold:

- remaining Yuan Qi
- converted True Qi

until conversion completes.

## Core unlocks

- True Qi resource
- natural cultivation flight
- advanced Secret Techniques
- greatly improved combat efficiency
- more complex barriers
- much stronger weapon infusion

## Physical evolution

Body becomes increasingly True-Qi reinforced.

## Flight

First realm where all normal cultivators can fly through cultivation alone.

Flight cost depends on:

- True Qi
- Power %
- movement mastery
- technique
- body/weight/artifacts

# 22. Dao Seal

Source anchor:

A Dao Seal is formed by condensing the cultivator's lifetime:

- Martial practice
- energies
- foundation

**Dao Seal.**

- Primary Grand Dao

A Dao Seal cultivator is significantly stronger than a normal Pseudo-Great Emperor due to masterful primary-Principle control.

# 23. Half-Step Open Heaven

Source anchor:

After Dao Seal, cultivators refine:

- Yin
- Yang
- Wood
- Fire
- Earth
- Metal
- Water

Five Elements must normally follow a generating cycle according to starting element.

After 5 strengths are condensed, the cultivator is called Half-Step Open Heaven and gains embryonic World Force.

## Element cycle

Normal Five-Element cycles:

- Wood → Fire → Earth → Metal → Water
- Fire → Earth → Metal → Water → Wood
- Earth → Metal → Water → Wood → Fire
- Metal → Water → Wood → Fire → Earth
- Water → Wood → Fire → Earth → Metal

Yin/Yang are handled separately.

## Resource grade

Every refined Open Heaven resource has a rank.

The player's eventual initial Open Heaven rank is determined by the **lowest-ranked** of the seven resources.

## Safety

Dao Seal stability matters.

Trying to refine a resource beyond what the Dao Seal can bear can:

- crack the Seal
- cause permanent foundation damage
- kill the cultivator

## Affinity interaction

High affinity makes refinement safer/easier.

Low affinity does not make it impossible, but requires:

- stronger resources
- better methods
- better Seal
- formation/alchemist support
- more preparation

# 24. Open Heaven — 9 Ranks

Source anchor:

Requirements:

- all seven Yin-Yang + Five Element strengths refined
- formation of a **Small Universe**

Primary power:

- **World Force**

## Small Universe

New persistent world-like internal system:

- background
- World Force
- size
- stability
- World Principles
- elemental balance
- living-creature support
- resource production
- time flow (future advanced mechanic)

## Initial rank

Determined by lowest rank among the seven refined resources.

Example:

- six Rank-7 resources
- one Rank-4 resource

Result:

- initial 4th Rank Open Heaven

High resources still improve background and may grant special comprehension, but cannot erase the lowest-resource bottleneck.

## Ordinary promotion limit

Normal Open Heaven cultivation has a roughly **two-rank promotion limit from initial rank** unless extraordinary methods/resources break the limit.

## Rank groups

### Low Rank — 1st to 3rd

Small Universe:

- formed
- generates World Force
- cannot normally support living populations

### Middle Rank — 4th to 6th

Massively stronger World Force/background.

Still not a fully living internal Universe World.

### High Rank — 7th to 9th

At 7th Rank:

- Small Universe World Principles become complete
- Small Universe becomes a true Universe World
- living creatures can grow/prosper within it

The gap from 6th → 7th is enormous.

The gaps:

- 7 → 8
- 8 → 9

are also extreme.

## Internal rank layers

Each rank should have:

- Newly Promoted
- Established
- Peak

Progress depends mainly on accumulating **Small Universe Background**, not ordinary comprehension XP.

## Background acquisition

Future methods:

- Open Heaven Pills
- World Fruits
- rare Universe Furnace pills
- Small Universe cultivation
- living-population feedback at High Rank
- exceptional treasures

# 25. World Creation

Source anchor:

Known requirements include extraordinary mastery of the myriad/3,000 Grand Daos and transcending the taboo/restrictions of the world.

Capabilities include creation of true Universe Worlds and life.

A former Open Heaven cultivator no longer fundamentally needs the Small Universe in the same way and may externalize it as a true Universe World.

## Myriad Ascension implementation

World Creation is not:

> Open Heaven Rank 10.

It is a fundamentally different state.

Core unlocks:

- perfect/myriad Dao integration
- Creation Authority
- Universe World creation
- life creation
- independent law creation
- externalization of former Small Universe
- ability to create permanent dimensions/worlds

World Creation should be the current final cultivation state.

# 26. Factors Beyond Realm

Realm is the largest single factor, but not the only one.

## Cultivation Method

Affects:

- progression speed
- energy purity
- stat development
- realm ceiling
- breakthrough stability
- special mechanics

## Technique Mastery

A high-mastery technique can outperform a low-mastery superior technique.

## Power %

Controls current output and cost.

Higher Power:

- stronger technique
- higher speed
- higher defense
- higher Qi use
- higher strain

## Purity

High purity:

- better energy efficiency
- stronger breakthroughs
- less backlash
- higher effective combat power

## Impurities

Sources:

- poor pills
- bad environments
- contaminated Qi
- rushed cultivation

## Bloodline

Can provide:

- physical strength
- lifespan
- resistance
- affinity
- special abilities
- realm-scale combat advantages

## Physique

Separate from bloodline.

Can influence:

- training
- elemental compatibility
- body techniques
- breakthrough requirements

## Artifacts

A high-grade artifact can greatly increase threat.

But usage is limited by:

- Strength
- Agility
- Qi output
- control
- Spiritual Sense

## Formations

Can bridge realm gaps through preparation.

## Environment

Can modify:

- absorption
- affinity
- technique strength
- recovery
- contamination

## Pills

Can accelerate:

- accumulation
- healing
- conversion
- breakthroughs

But poor use creates impurities/dependency.

## Comprehension

Required increasingly at high realms.

Raw energy alone cannot create:

- Shi
- Domain
- Principle mastery
- Dao Seal
- World Creation

## Willpower

Important for:

- Shi
- Demonic control
- World Baptism
- Qi deviation
- soul defense
- breakthroughs

## Battle experience

Improves:

- technique mastery
- battle comprehension
- timing
- adaptation

It does not substitute for all cultivation.

# 27. Universal Breakthrough Checklist

Every major breakthrough evaluates:

## Required

- realm progress
- energy requirement
- body requirement
- comprehension requirement
- method support

## Stability

- injury
- meridian damage
- soul damage
- impurity
- contamination
- fatigue
- lifespan condition

## Special

Examples:

- Qi Transformation: World Baptism
- True Element: Yuan → True Qi conversion
- Immortal Ascension: Knowledge Sea formation
- Saint: True → Saint Qi conversion
- Origin Returning: Shi
- Origin King: Domain
- Dao Source: Source Qi + Principles
- Emperor: Emperor Qi + Principle control
- Dao Seal: Grand Dao condensation
- Half-Step OH: five strengths
- Open Heaven: seven strengths + Small Universe
- World Creation: myriad Dao completion/transcendence

A breakthrough can therefore fail even at 100% ordinary progress.

# 28. Minecraft Numerical Implementation

Do not give players billions of hearts.

Use:

- modest visible HP scaling
- cultivation defense
- damage penetration
- Realm Power Tier comparison
- technique coefficients
- resistance/suppression

Suggested damage pipeline:

1. raw technique damage
2. Power % scaling
3. technique mastery
4. stat scaling
5. affinity/environment
6. artifact
7. realm-power comparison
8. target defense/body grade
9. special penetration
10. final Minecraft damage

This lets a Saint feel impossibly stronger than a Mortal without overflowing Minecraft's numeric/UI systems.
