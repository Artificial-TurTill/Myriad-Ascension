# Empire Politics

Empires are political factions with their own diplomacy, succession, and state policy.

They are not interchangeable with cultivation-path affiliation.

## Imperial Technique Trait

An Imperial technique can simultaneously carry another path trait.

Examples:

- Imperial + Righteous
- Imperial + Buddhist
- Imperial + Demonic
- Imperial + Unorthodox
- Imperial + Neutral

The Imperial portion grants a political/combat bonus only when:

- the user belongs to the relevant Empire,
- the target is an enemy declared by that Empire,
- and the technique itself carries the Imperial trait.

## Diplomacy

The future political screen must allow the Emperor to define at minimum:

- allies,
- enemies,
- war state,
- state religion,
- major internal appointments.

Political relations are Empire-specific rather than hard-coded solely from broad cultivation affiliations.

## State Religion

An Empire can define a state religion/tradition.

For the current Asian-focused development scope, the initial supported religious policy is limited.

Christianity/Westernized religious content is explicitly deferred until separately designed.

Where an Empire has an exclusive state religion, rival mutually-exclusive religions are not simultaneously treated as the official state religion.

## Player Ascension to the Throne

A player cannot become Emperor simply because the Imperial family dies to random Minecraft hazards.

Deaths such as:

- falling,
- lava,
- burning,
- drowning,
- suffocation,
- ordinary natural/environmental causes,

do **not** open the player-usurpation route.

For player usurpation to become valid:

1. the Emperor must fall in a qualifying political/enemy battle,
2. the Crown Prince must also fall in a qualifying political/enemy battle,
3. the player must become the strongest eligible cultivator in the Empire.

The implementation must retain death context so succession cannot be triggered by intentionally pushing rulers into environmental accidents and treating that as legitimate conquest.

## Future Succession

Normal hereditary succession after non-political deaths remains an NPC-dynasty mechanic to be designed separately.

The rule above specifically governs the route by which a player can seize/ascend the throne.
