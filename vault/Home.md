# Myriad Ascension Vault

Two mods for **NeoForge 1.21.1**, private use. Repository: `Artificial-TurTill/Myriad-Ascension`.

| Mod | Jar | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Ascension Foundation** (`ascension_foundation`) | `ascension_foundation-<version>.jar` | The engine extracted from Dragonblock C+ (the JRMCore layer): character, races and appearance, power types, attributes and statistics, classes, TP, power release, skills, transformations, Body-as-life combat, creation wizard, character sheet, HUD. |
| **Myriad Realm** (`myriad_realm`) | `myriad_realm-<version>.jar` | The cultivation add-on. Requires the Foundation. Adds the Qi power type (own system, every attribute starts at 1), its classes, skills and transformations. |

Install both jars; Myriad Realm refuses to load without the Foundation.
