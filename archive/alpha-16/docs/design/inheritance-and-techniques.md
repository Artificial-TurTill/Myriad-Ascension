# Inheritances, Manuals, Technique Signatures, and Qi Nature

## Access to Cultivation

A Mortal does not automatically receive cultivation.

Valid acquisition routes include:

- a Master,
- a Clan,
- a Sect,
- a Cultivator Tomb,
- a Monument,
- other inheritance/loot structures.

A manual or inheritance is not necessarily universal. It can have:

- affinity requirements,
- body/polarity requirements,
- bloodline requirements,
- moral or Karma requirements,
- affiliation requirements,
- realm limits,
- technique prerequisites,
- structure-specific trials.

## Tombs

Cultivator Tombs can contain partial or complete inheritances.

The tomb's inheritance does not have to suit the player. A player may obtain a method that is inefficient or incompatible with their current build.

A cultivation method can also have a maximum supported realm. Reaching that ceiling means the player must:

- switch methods,
- extend/repair the inheritance,
- obtain a continuation,
- create a new method,
- or use another valid progression route.

## Monuments

Monuments are selective inheritance sources.

Elemental monuments can require a minimum affinity.

Requirements can explicitly test either:

- **Innate potential** — what the player was born with,
- **Current attunement** — what the player has trained/refined into.

Yin/Yang monuments judge the sex-aligned natural polarity:

- Male -> Yang potential,
- Female -> Yin potential.

The opposite starting polarity remains at 1 during genesis and receives no bonus points. Later systems may alter current attunement, but this does not rewrite innate potential.

## Technique Signatures

Techniques carry independent sets of traits.

### Path traits

- Neutral
- Righteous
- Demonic
- Buddhist
- Unorthodox
- Imperial

A technique can carry more than one path trait. Examples:

- Imperial + Righteous
- Imperial + Buddhist
- Imperial + Demonic
- Neutral + Earth

Imperial is therefore not mutually exclusive with the other cultivation traditions.

### Elemental traits

The signature can also contain:

- Wood
- Fire
- Earth
- Metal
- Water
- Yin
- Yang

Additional elements/energies can be added later without collapsing them into affiliation.

### Nature traits

Current technical tags include:

- Harmonizing
- Violent
- Poison
- Blood
- Life Drain
- Gluttony
- Desire
- Soul
- Body Tempering
- Longevity
- Defensive
- Concealment

These are mechanical descriptors, not moral labels.

## Qi Nature Imprinting

Practicing a technique can leave its path nature in the cultivator's Qi.

Examples:

- Demonic practice leaves a Demonic trait that can make the cultivator's Qi harmful/hostile to others.
- Buddhist practice can leave harmonizing/benevolent characteristics.
- Righteous arts need not inherently carry either Demonic corruption or Karma-based Buddhist traits.
- Unorthodox arts are often violent and may use poison, extreme appetite, desire, theft, or other socially dangerous mechanisms.
- Imperial arts can coexist with another path nature and receive political bonuses under the empire's rules.

The player's **official membership** and **actual Qi nature** are deliberately separate.

A Righteous sect member can therefore secretly carry Demonic Qi until exposed.

## Exposure

Burst, Qi techniques, weapon infusion, cultivation, and other active Qi use expose the **traits actually present in the Qi being used**.

Concealment can suppress or falsify that signature.

This is separate from the player's official faction membership and reputation.

## Desire-type Arts

Some Unorthodox/vice-based arts can drain the opposite polarity.

For NPCs this may directly reduce their Yin/Yang statistic.

For players, polarity drained by such an effect is recoverable through cultivation and Qi circulation rather than being a permanent deletion of the character's innate potential.

## Imperial Techniques

Imperial techniques receive their political bonus only when:

- the user belongs to the relevant Empire,
- the technique carries the Imperial trait,
- and the target is an enemy declared by that Empire.

The future Empire political screen will control allies, enemies, religion, succession, and related state policy.
